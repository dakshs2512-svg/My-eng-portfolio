02 / The person behind the pixels

Creative at heart.
Curious by nature.

DS
DAKSH SAXENAEST. ALWAYS

I like the space where design, technology, and storytelling overlap.

I’m a CSE student and independent creator who enjoys turning ideas into things people can see, use, and feel. From editing videos and shaping motion to designing interfaces and building for the web, I’m drawn to the details that make an experience memorable.

My approach is equal parts experimentation and intention: understand the problem, find the visual language, then keep refining until everything feels like it belongs. I’m interested in work that is useful, expressive, and just a little unexpected.

03 / How I work

Make it mean
something.

Good work is rarely a straight line. This is the loose framework I use to turn a first thought into a considered result.

01 — DISCOVER

Start with why.

Get curious, ask better questions, and find the real idea hiding underneath the first idea.

02 — EXPLORE

Find the feeling.

Sketch, test, prototype, and try unexpected directions until the concept has its own voice.

03 — REFINE

Make it click.

Bring the details together, remove what gets in the way, and make the final experience feel effortless.
